About Me

The Intersection of Trade and Tech

I’m Favour Ifeanyi Ugochukwu, known professionally as Mr Ifeanyi. Based in Nigeria, I work at the intersection of international trade, business operations, and technology, building practical connections between markets, people, and digital systems.

Through Alteon Exports, I facilitate the sourcing and export of African raw materials while helping businesses navigate international procurement and connect with supply opportunities. My focus is on building dependable trade relationships through clear communication, responsible sourcing, and a commitment to quality.

Through Kairos AI Systems, I help businesses solve operational challenges through custom software development, web applications, and AI-powered automation. From improving digital experiences to reducing repetitive work and streamlining business processes, the goal is simple: build technology that solves real problems and creates measurable value.

My work is guided by a straightforward belief: business should move with less friction, technology should solve meaningful problems, and opportunities should not be limited by borders.

Whether I’m facilitating a trade transaction, developing a digital solution, or exploring a better way to solve a business challenge, I bring a practical mindset, a commitment to execution, and a long-term view of growth.
